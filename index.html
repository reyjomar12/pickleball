About Pickleball

At SawaDika Pickleball Club, we are passionate about community, health, and inclusive play. Based on our 3-court setup (as seen on our Home page), we provide a vibrant space for all skill levels to connect and grow. Experience the fun, energy, and social spirit of the world's fastest-growing sport.

WHAT IS PICKLEBALL?

A paddle sport combining elements of tennis, badminton, and ping-pong, played on a smaller court with a perforated ball.

3.

Features three professional-grade indoor courts (seen on our Home page) and a comfortable pro-shop and player lounge.
